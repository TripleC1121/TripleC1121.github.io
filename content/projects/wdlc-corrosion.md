## 研究問題
在 316L 不鏽鋼上鍍鎢摻雜類鑽碳（W-DLC）薄膜之前，先做電漿氮化，能不能提升抗腐蝕性？

## 我做了什麼
- 設計比較組別：有無氮化中間層的 W-DLC 薄膜（鍍膜與氮化由金屬工業研究發展中心執行）
- 以動電位極化與電化學阻抗譜（EIS）評估抗腐蝕性
- 以 Raman（ID/IG）與接觸角分析薄膜結構與表面性質

## 結果
氮化中間層沒有提升抗腐蝕性，反而使腐蝕電位降低、電流密度升高、阻抗下降。推論原因是氮化後表面多孔，使薄膜附著性變差。

## Question
Does plasma nitriding 316L stainless steel before depositing a tungsten-doped diamond-like carbon (W-DLC) film improve corrosion resistance?

## What I did
- Designed the comparison: W-DLC films with and without a nitrided interlayer (deposition and nitriding by the Metal Industries Research & Development Centre)
- Evaluated corrosion resistance by potentiodynamic polarization and electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS)
- Analyzed film structure and surface properties by Raman (ID/IG) and contact angle

## Result
The nitrided interlayer did not help. It lowered the corrosion potential, raised the current density, and reduced impedance, likely because the porous nitrided surface weakened film adhesion.
