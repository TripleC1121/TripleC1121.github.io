## 為什麼做這個題目
傳統骨科植入物常遇到癒合延遲的問題。壓電材料能在受力時產生微小電訊號，模擬骨組織的電機械特性。

## 我做了什麼
- 以靜電紡絲、退火與極化製備 PVDF 奈米纖維膜，並加入 20 wt% BaTiO3 奈米顆粒提升壓電性
- 量測纖維形貌、β 相比例（FTIR）、孔隙率、拉伸強度、接觸角與 d33
- 以 MC3T3-E1 前驅成骨細胞進行體外實驗，並在大鼠股骨缺損模型中評估（與骨科醫師合作）

## 結果
- 纖維直徑 90–200 nm，β 相比例 43–48%，d33 為 56–60 pC/N，拉伸強度約 25 MPa
- 體外：21 天鈣沉積提升 1.5–2 倍
- 體內：60 天後三點彎曲最大破壞載重，PVDF 塗層組 150 ± 20 N，未塗層對照組 120 ± 15 N（p = 0.045）

## 限制
動物實驗樣本數少，仍需更大規模的驗證。成果已被 ORS 2026 年會接受。

## Why this problem
Conventional orthopedic implants can suffer from delayed healing. Piezoelectric materials generate small electrical signals under load, mimicking the electromechanical behavior of bone.

## What I did
- Fabricated PVDF nanofiber films by electrospinning, annealing, and poling, with 20 wt% BaTiO3 nanoparticles added to raise piezoelectricity
- Measured fiber morphology, β-phase fraction (FTIR), porosity, tensile strength, contact angle, and d33
- Ran in vitro tests with MC3T3-E1 preosteoblasts and an in vivo rat femoral defect study (with orthopedic surgeons)

## Results
- Fiber diameter 90–200 nm, β-phase 43–48%, d33 56–60 pC/N, tensile strength about 25 MPa
- In vitro: 1.5–2× calcium deposition at 21 days
- In vivo: maximum failure load at 60 days was 150 ± 20 N for PVDF-coated pins versus 120 ± 15 N for uncoated controls (p = 0.045)

## Limitations
The animal sample size was small and larger studies are needed. Accepted at the ORS 2026 Annual Meeting.
