## 為什麼做這個題目
骨頭本身具有壓電特性，受力時會產生電訊號並促進骨修復。這個研究想讓金屬植入物表面也具備同樣的能力。常見的 BaTiO3 鍍膜方法需要 800 °C 以上的高溫，但 Ti-6Al-4V 在高溫下會氧化，導致塗層剝落。

## 我做了什麼
- 建立四站整合製程：陽極處理形成 TiO2 前驅層 → 180 °C 水熱合成 → Ar–4%H2 真空退火 → 電暈極化
- 比較 3 種前驅層厚度、不同 Ba(OH)2 與 KOH 濃度、4 個退火溫度（400–1000 °C）與 2 種極化方式
- 以 SEM/EDX、XRD、Raman 分析形貌、成分與晶相，以 PFM 量測局部壓電響應
- 自行架設電暈極化設備

## 結果
- 最佳條件：0.5 M Ba(OH)2 + 1.5 M KOH 水熱反應，搭配 600 °C 退火
- 局部 d33 由 66.95 提升至 98.65 pm/V
- 800 °C 以上退火會造成基材氧化與塗層剝落
- 本研究採用的極化條件未能進一步提升壓電響應，是後續要優化的方向

## 學到的事
薄膜的晶相判讀不能只靠單一儀器：XRD 沒有看到四方相的峰分裂，但 Raman 清楚顯示四方相特徵，需要交叉驗證。

## Why this problem
Bone is piezoelectric: mechanical loading produces electrical signals that promote repair. This work aims to give metal implants the same ability. Common BaTiO3 coating methods need temperatures above 800 °C, but Ti-6Al-4V oxidizes at high temperature and the coating delaminates.

## What I did
- Built a four-step process: anodization to form a TiO2 precursor layer → hydrothermal synthesis at 180 °C → vacuum annealing in Ar–4%H2 → corona poling
- Compared 3 precursor thicknesses, Ba(OH)2 and KOH concentrations, 4 annealing temperatures (400–1000 °C), and 2 poling modes
- Characterized morphology, composition, and phase with SEM/EDX, XRD, and Raman; local piezoelectric response measured by PFM
- Built the corona-poling setup

## Results
- Best condition: 0.5 M Ba(OH)2 + 1.5 M KOH hydrothermal reaction with annealing at 600 °C
- Local d33 increased from 66.95 to 98.65 pm/V
- Annealing at 800 °C and above caused substrate oxidation and coating delamination
- The poling conditions used did not further improve the response; this is the next thing to optimize

## What I learned
Phase identification in thin coatings should not rely on one instrument: XRD showed no tetragonal peak splitting, while Raman clearly showed tetragonal features. Cross-validation was necessary.
